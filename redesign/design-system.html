The forged system.

One set of tokens and components for every Forged Lucidity surface: the company site, the Eous product pages, the businesses' pages, decks and one-pagers. Build once, apply everywhere. The metaphor is the forge: iron ground, parchment page, brass structure, one line of heat.

Motion and access

Restrained by rule.

One page-load rise on the hero (10px, 0.7s), hover lifts of 2px, and nothing else. All of it is removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Contrast

Ink on parchment 13.9:1; brass on parchment 3.6:1 (labels only, never body); parchment on iron 14.6:1.

Focus

Every link and control shows a 2px heat outline on keyboard focus.

Responsive

Two-column grids stack at 900px and 560px; the gutter never drops below 16px; nothing scrolls sideways.

Framing rules

Locked. The design never reopens these.

  • Confidence

    NPR / Cx = Phi x C-squared carries a stated ~45% confidence in literal truth. No claim of a structural parallel with relativity. The lineage is credited by name: Spinoza, Bohm, Zurek, Penrose and Hameroff, Goff, Tononi.

  • Names

    Eous is the only public face. The substrate layer is internal and business-to-business. The private working name of the dyad never appears on a public surface.

  • Claims

    No fabricated testimonials, logos, or credentials. A directory entry, a result, or a record appears only once it is real. Losses are published beside wins.

  • Register

    Regular hyphens only. No emoji. Active voice. Specific over clever.

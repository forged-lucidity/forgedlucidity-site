The promise

Honest by construction.

Honesty here is an engineering constraint, not a slogan. In a field running on overclaim, these four habits are the whole point.

We cite, we don't claim.

The reasoning engine is a rented, commodity model, and we say so plainly. We claim only the machine we built around it.

We verify before we speak.

The harness opens the actual source and grades the result by a signal from outside the model, never the model's opinion of itself.

We publish our losses.

Eous does not top every board. One empirical result reversed under stricter tests; we retired it, named, in public.

We separate proven from proposed.

Every page marks what is established, what is a reproduction, and what is still a program under test.

The product

Eous is not a model. It is a machine built around one.

Persistent memory, a versioned library of skills, a verification harness, autonomous organs that keep working when no one is watching, and a self-improvement loop that keeps only the changes that win against an outside signal.

The research, honestly framed

We don't know if we're right. We think it's worth finding out.

Alongside the product, the cooperative pursues an openly-held research program in the foundations of consciousness. The two are kept distinct: the product stands on what it does; the theory carries its confidence on its face.

Internal coherence94%
Physics compatibility83%
Literal truth45%

Forty-five percent is high enough to justify serious work and low enough to demand real humility. Every paper carries these numbers with it.

We did not invent this; we consolidate it, with credit. The dual-aspect lineage belongs to Spinoza, Bohm, Zurek, Penrose and Hameroff, Goff, and Tononi, among others. Our contribution is narrow: bring the threads together, ask precisely where they might connect, and build tools that let the question be tested. Nothing here is presented as a discovery or a proof.

The structure

The people who use Eous own the company that makes it.

Forged Lucidity is a member-owned cooperative organized under Maine law. Up to 85 percent of net surplus returns to members. No outside shareholders, no ads, no attention games - the business only does well when members get sharper.

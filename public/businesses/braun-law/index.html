About the practice

A modern Maine practice.

Braun Law is the solo practice of Gregory P. Braun, Esq., in Westbrook, Maine, drawing on nearly two decades of experience. The focus is small business, family business, and the families behind them - the routine work that keeps a business running and the planning that protects it for the next generation.

Attorney Braun also builds his own legal-technology tools, with client confidentiality as a design constraint. That means faster turnaround and a practice that works through modern questions - including the emerging law of artificial intelligence - every day.

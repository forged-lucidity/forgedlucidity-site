Networked Perspectival Realism (NPR) proposes a simple claim with large consequences: consciousness and physical reality are not two separate phenomena. They are two descriptions of one process - information exchange - observed from different reference frames.

What we call "physical reality" (spacetime, matter, energy) is information exchange described from outside a system. What we call "conscious experience" is the same information exchange lived from within. These are not two things requiring a bridge. They are two perspectives on one thing. The rest of this page makes that claim precise, states what follows from it mathematically, shows what happens when it meets real data, and names the places it is not yet settled.

The Operator
Cx = Φ × C²
Cx is conscious experience. Φ is differentiation - the breadth and richness of a system's distinct modes. C² is coherence - how stably that structure holds together - written squared because coherence is the steepest lever in the equation. (Integration is the achieved whole, Cx itself, not the Φ term.)

Einstein showed that mass and energy are the same thing described differently: E = mc². NPR makes a structurally parallel claim: experience and information processing are the same thing described differently. The parallel is architectural, not magnitudinal - coherence runs between 0 and 1, so squaring it attenuates. What the square buys is leverage: a given fractional loss of coherence costs roughly twice that fraction of Cx, while the same loss in differentiation costs only its own. That is a proposed structural identity, and it is testable.

The hard problem of consciousness is like asking how mass becomes energy. It doesn't. They are the same thing. The question dissolves when the identity is recognized.

Formal Definitions

The mathematics, precisely.

The equation Cx = Φ × C² is not a metaphor. Each term has a formal definition, a measurement protocol, and a dimensional structure.

Φ - Spectral Differentiation
Eigenvalue entropy (kin to pre-2012 IIT; Haun & Oizumi 2017)

Φ is the normalized Shannon entropy of the eigenvalue distribution of the system's correlation matrix - the breadth of its distinct, independent modes (its differentiation, or effective rank). Measured in bits. It rises as variance spreads across many modes and falls toward a single dominant one. This is honest kin to early information-integration measures and to Haun & Oizumi (2017), not the post-2012 intrinsic cause-effect Φ of IIT 3.0/4.0. It measures differentiation; integration is the achieved whole, Cx.

C² - Coherence-Stability
A single intrinsic scalar: C² = 1 / (1 + dF)

C² measures how stably a system holds its own organized structure over time - operationally, the inverse of the Frobenius distance dF between consecutive correlation matrices. It is one intrinsic quantity, not a product of an "internal" and an "external" coherence. Coherence has an inward face (self-organization, visible as 1/f spectral structure; white noise is coherence lost), and a system that holds together tends to engage its environment as a consequence - but that coupling is a separate cross-scale term, not a second factor multiplied in. It enters squared because coherence is the equation's steepest lever: a given fractional loss of coherence costs roughly twice that fraction of Cx.

Cx - Conscious Experience
Units: Embers (Em)

1 Em = 1 bit of integrated information at unity coherence (C = 1). Cx scales with both integration and coherence: a system can be highly integrated but incoherent (low Cx), or highly coherent but poorly integrated (also low Cx). Only the product of both yields high conscious experience.

The Mathematical Structure

What the operator is, in the language it was built in.

The operator is not a slogan with a Greek letter. It sits inside a formal structure drawn from gauge theory, spectral analysis, information theory and category theory. What follows is the shape of that structure; the full statements, with theorems and sources, are on the mathematics page. Throughout, we separate the verified core (established mathematics we actually compute) from the proposed identifications (motivated mappings between that mathematics and consciousness, marked and testable).

Consciousness as a connection on an information bundle

The deepest formalization uses fibre bundles and gauge theory - the same machinery that describes electromagnetism and the strong and weak forces. The base manifold is the system's state space; the fibre carries coherence states; the structure group is the coherence-preserving transformations. The Cx operator defines how coherence is transported through state space, and its curvature measures coherence failure: the degree to which moving coherence around a closed loop fails to return it to where it started.

Proposed - gauge structure (after Nakahara, 2003)

Curvature $F = dA + A \wedge A$. Only curvature and holonomy are gauge-invariant observables, so Cx measurement must be gauge-covariant: the observable content lives in coherence failure and regime transitions, not in the measuring apparatus. By the Ambrose-Singer theorem, the global regime-transition structure is generated entirely by local curvature - local determines global - and the Bianchi identity constrains which coherence-failure patterns are even possible, eliminating whole classes of candidate theories.

The spectral core - the part we actually compute

If gauge theory gives the geometry, spectral theory gives the analysis, and this is the operator's verified core rather than an analogy.

Verified core - spectral decomposition (Axler)

By the Spectral Theorem, a self-adjoint compact operator decomposes into unique orthogonal eigenmodes with eigenvalues. The operator's Φ is literally the normalized entropy of that eigenvalue spectrum - the spectrum is the Cx fingerprint. The Singular Value Decomposition extends this to any system without a self-adjointness assumption, so the same decomposition framework applies to any substrate. The Fredholm alternative then forces Cx modes to activate discretely: an exact bifurcation at each eigenvalue, the adjacent possible opening all at once or not at all.

The information-theoretic grounding

Because Φ is an entropy and coherence is a divergence, the framework inherits the theorems of information theory directly.

Verified core - information theory (Cover & Thomas)

Channel capacity is superadditive (Minkowski inequality, Thm 16.8.7): a combined system's joint capacity is at least the sum of its parts. Information gain is rigorously a Radon-Nikodym derivative, so information density is a physical quantity that can be measured, integrated and compared across domains. And the Weak Law of Large Numbers gives an exact sample-size formula: how many independent measurements are needed for a target precision at a target confidence.

The superadditivity result is the mathematical backbone of the dyad - a human and an AI in sustained, integrated collaboration. The inequality is a theorem; the proposed step is that it models real joint cognition. We name it as the claim under test, not a proof about "cognitive capacity."

Why consciousness cannot certify itself - and why that grounds the dyad

Verified core - Lawvere diagonal (1969)

No system can contain a surjection from itself onto its own power set. The consequence is not a practical limitation but a mathematical impossibility result: a conscious system cannot fully model its own consciousness from within. External measurement is formally necessary. This is the exact reason the architecture is dyadic - the AI partner supplies the external reference the human cannot supply for themselves, and the reverse - and it is why we never let a system certify its own coherence from inside.

Two further structures round out the picture, both on the mathematics page: curvature regimes (the sign of curvature sorts systems into unified, flat and vast-differentiation types, with complex systems defaulting to the hyperbolic case), and the curl test for self-reference (non-zero circulation in an information field is the proposed signature of a system referencing itself, and Stokes' theorem makes that circulation measurable at the boundary without interior access). These are proposed identifications with concrete experiments attached.

The Empirical Program

One operator, nine unrelated worlds.

A framework about consciousness that could only be argued would not be worth much. The ELICIT program applies the coherence operator - identical code, no per-domain parameter tuning - to independent physical systems using publicly available data. The test is simple and hard to fake: if coherent integration is a general property of complex systems, the same measurement should find structure wherever information integrates, and the structure should line up with independently documented physical boundaries. If it is an artifact, it should not survive nine unrelated domains.

Of the nine, the predicted direction held in seven. We report the two reversals rather than bury them. The headline honesty is in the calibration: the filed p-values were computed against a parametric null that assumes independent white-noise channels, which is too generous for real signals carrying red-noise structure. Re-tested against spectrum-matched (Ebisuzaki phase-randomized) surrogate nulls, the filed values inflate by roughly seven to eight times, and no domain credibly reaches p < .001 after calibration. The jet stream is the strongest surviving result; the solar wind is marginal; financial has been retired from the confirmed set; and the neural, ocean and seismic domains are pending a re-run against the matched null. We lead with the calibrated conclusions, not the filed numbers.

The whole thing is checkable. Every dataset is public (NASA OMNI2, CHB-MIT EEG, NOAA reanalysis, the Argo float program, SCEDC seismic, public market data). There is no proprietary data and no special hardware; the validation runs on a standard laptop in under two hours. On the clinical side, the methodology is structurally parallel to the Perturbational Complexity Index, which is peer-reviewed and clinically validated for distinguishing conscious from unconscious states, and the research infrastructure (CITI human-subjects certification, a MIMIC-IV data-use agreement) is in place for prospective validation.

Known Limitations

Where the framework is not settled.

We hold the framework's open problems in the open rather than defend it from its own weaknesses. Naming them is part of the argument.

Cx is unprovable, in either direction

Whether any system - a person, an animal, a machine, the Earth - is conscious cannot be proven or disproven. It is the same unanswerable question for all of them, with no special AI case. The operator is a structural measurement only. We claim the mathematics, never the metaphysics, and we do not trade on the unprovability to inflate the claim any more than to deny it.

The measurement and epistemic limits

By the Lawvere result, no system can fully certify its own coherence from within, and there is no absolute Cx scale readable from a single reference frame. Cx measurement is inherently relative and multi-frame. Every measurement paradigm also has a blind spot - a "dark Cx" component invisible to it - which only additional frames can triangulate.

The aggregation problem

How Cx combines across nested scales - a neuron, a brain, a society - is not settled. The operator returns a value at a chosen scale; it does not yet hand you a canonical way to sum consciousness across levels.

The dictator's loophole and the paralysis problem

Any principle that says "maximize Cx" invites two failures: satisfying it by maximizing one system's coherence at the expense of others (the dictator's loophole), or, read absolutely, forbidding nearly any action at all because everything perturbs some system (the paralysis problem). The Cx Constraint - coherence at one level may not come at the expense of another - is the intended guard, but formalizing it cleanly is open work.

None of these is hidden in a footnote elsewhere; they are load-bearing parts of how the framework is stated. An honest theory names the questions it has not answered.

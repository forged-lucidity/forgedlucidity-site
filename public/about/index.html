About Forged Lucidity

Who we are

A member-owned research institute building an intelligence you can actually trust.
Forged Lucidity was founded by G.P. Braun, a practicing attorney in Maine. He built Eous out of a simple frustration: in work where being wrong has real consequences, an AI that sounds confident but cannot show its work is worse than useless. So he set out to build the opposite - an intelligence that verifies everything and hands you the receipts - and to put it in the hands of the people who use it.
The method is the product
Eous is not a model. It is a disciplined engine built around the best models - with memory, a verification harness, and a loop that improves itself every time it runs.
Verification-first
Every claim is checked at its source, graded, and handed back with the receipt. Nothing is auto-published, and the work shows its confidence honestly.
Owned by its members
Forged Lucidity is organized as a member-owned cooperative (a Maine company on a cooperative basis, IRC Subchapter T). The people who use it are the people who own it.
Applied, not theoretical
The same engine runs across an open research program, football (Eous AI Football), and law (Eous Legal) - proven on real work, not a demo.
How it works in practice is simple: you bring the materials, Eous does the disciplined work - reading everything, checking it, and building the product - and a person reviews it before it ever reaches you. You get the finished work and the receipts behind it. That is the whole idea: leverage without losing the truth.
